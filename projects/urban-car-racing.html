Urban Car Racing

Android
Offline vs AI 3D Car Racing Drift & Nitro
My Role in Project
  • Developed a 3D arcade street racing game in Unity (URP) set in a full city, with a career map of 6 levels across three level types — Race, Time and Key.
  • Built a custom car controller with drifting, body sway, tire smoke, skid marks and a nitro boost system, with per-car stats driven by ScriptableObjects.
  • Created AI opponents that follow racing lines, avoid walls with a raycast sensor ring, brake for turns and U-turns, and match their pace to the player by difficulty.
  • Added AI stuck recovery — reversing out of walls, and respawning at the next checkpoint if still stuck.
  • Implemented a checkpoint race system with live positions, a guide arrow, a finish board, and a circular minimap showing the road network, route, player and AI cars.
  • Built a garage with 5 cars, an orbit camera to inspect each car, and paint customization.
  • Designed progression with coins based on finishing position, up to 5 stars per level, and saved career progress.
  • Added mobile touch controls with auto throttle and steering sensitivity, a dynamic chase camera with look-behind, speed lines, and platform-tuned post-processing.
